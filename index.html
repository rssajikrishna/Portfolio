What I work on

From raw tables to an answer a brand team can trust

Pipelines

Delta Live Tables with data quality expectations. Failing records are quarantined before they reach brand KPIs.

Governance

Unity Catalog access controls and Volumes for documents, plus AI Gateway token budgets and rate limits so usage and cost stay predictable.

Semantic layer

Metric views for NBRx, naive patient starts and length of therapy, and a Genie space grounded with verified SQL, so a metric means one thing whether a person or an LLM asks for it.

Agents

A planner routes each question to a Genie text-to-SQL agent, a Vector Search retrieval agent over clinical and competitive documents, or both.

Evaluation

MLflow tracing on every step, and LLM judges with custom scorers that catch routing and grounding regressions before release.
